Sophämtning shows when your household waste is collected at your Swedish address, and lets you use it in your flows. Add your address by searching for it, and the device shows the next collection, days left, what is collected tomorrow and one row per waste type. Get a reminder the evening before or on the morning of a collection, check whether a waste type is collected today, tomorrow or in a number of days, and be told when the schedule changes, for example around holidays. A dashboard widget shows the next collections in the colour of each waste type.

The data comes from Sopor, a free and unofficial service that reads the municipalities' public collection schedules. It is not affiliated with any municipality or waste company. In case of differences, the information from your municipality always applies.
